# Monopoly lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **A small market to own first.** "Members-only, green, on its own open land, in Thaltej, for ages 19 to 46" is a narrow, nameable niche. If a capped membership (structures E/F) fills, the café starts with a large share of a small market, which is the lens's ideal opening.
- **A possible brand and community moat.** A community where members bring members, plus a founders' wall and member events, is the closest thing a café gets to a network effect. Brand is the one durable advantage this lens thinks a small business can realistically build.
- **Prepaid cash and lock-in.** Stored-value cards pull cash forward and raise switching costs: a member with ₹15,000 on the card has no reason to try the café next door.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Competing in a crowded market, with nothing proprietary.** Food, drink and a garden setting are not ten times better than the alternatives. How many open-air or "aesthetic" cafés already serve this buyer near Thaltej is unknown. If there are many, members-only becomes a barrier to entry and not an advantage. Open question for /founder-competitors.
2. **Copied by month six.** A competitor with land can add a garden, a loyalty card and members' nights. Exclusivity holds only if the community is real, not just the gate. Nothing in the brief yet stops a copycat.
3. **Distribution and timing are undefined.** No plan for how the first 100 members arrive or what each costs, and no "why now". Whether 19–46 buyers in Thaltej will prepay to enter a café at all is unproven. Open question for /founder-consumer. Whether the land cost lets a capped membership pay its way is also unknown. Open question for /founder-cfo.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What is the secret: what do you know about this buyer that existing cafés nearby have missed?
- Who else sells this to the same person within a 15-minute drive, and why would they pay to be locked in to you?
- How do the first 100 members arrive, through whom, and at what cost each?
- What stops a well-funded café chain copying the format in six months?
- What will this place own in ten years that a newcomer cannot buy: the land, the community, the brand?
- Why now, and why you as the team (food, hospitality, community-building)?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. /founder-competitors shows a real gap: no direct members-only or garden-community café already owns this buyer nearby.
2. Pre-sell a capped founding cohort (structure E) before building. Set the target with /founder-cfo, and fill most of it as proof of demand and of a distribution channel.
3. Name one durable advantage to build on purpose (most likely community plus brand, possibly owned land), with a concrete plan for how members recruit members.
