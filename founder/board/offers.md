# Offers lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Cash arrives before the coffee is made.** A stored-value card with a joining fee (structures A, C, E) brings money in upfront, and a capped founding round (E) gives real scarcity, not invented scarcity.
- **The ladder is already there.** The bonus tiers (Seed to Forest) and segment tiers (F) are the start of a good/better/best stack that rewards commitment.
- **Speed of result is high.** A member gets the result, a place to belong, on the first visit. Time-to-value is not the problem.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The market's pain is a want, not a pain.** "Somewhere nice to sit" is not a big pain, and 19 to 46 covers two very different buyers: a student and a 42-year-old professional. Whether there are enough buyers with spare money within reach of Thaltej is unknown. Open question for **/founder-consumer**.
2. **The effort and sacrifice are front-loaded and heavy.** The buyer pays a fee they cannot spend, locks money onto a single-venue card, faces a minimum-spend penalty, and gets nothing until the café exists. That is a lot to ask before there is any proof. On the value equation, likelihood (no proof) and effort (high) are both weak.
3. **Without a sharp outcome, it is a commodity with a gate.** "Eco, pure, happiness, creativity, freedom" are values, not an outcome a buyer pays for. If the menu and space are not clearly better than nearby cafés, the members-only rule becomes a reason to go elsewhere. How many alternatives there are and what they charge is unknown. Open question for **/founder-competitors**.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence, what does a member get that they cannot get at the café down the road?
- Which one segment is first: students and creators, professionals, families, or companies?
- Why would someone believe the experience is worth prepaying for before it opens? What proof can you show them: a pop-up, a pilot, or photos of the land?
- What is the guarantee? For example, could unspent balance be refunded in the first 90 days?
- What do fixed costs, gross margin and build cost come to, so that J and M are based on real numbers rather than placeholders? (**/founder-cfo**)
- Could you double the joining fee and still be the obvious choice? If not, what is missing from the stack?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Pick one beachhead segment** and write the dream outcome for that segment in a single sentence, with no list of values.
2. **Make the offer easier to accept:** start with structure B or C (all or most of the money is spendable credit), add a written refund guarantee on unspent balance, and use only a gentle quarterly minimum (D).
3. **Get proof before the build:** a founding-member pre-sale (E) with a real price and a hard cap. Treat a target number of paid founders as the go/no-go signal, sized by **/founder-cfo**.
4. **Run /founder-competitors and /founder-consumer** before you fix any rupee figure.
