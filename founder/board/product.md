# Product lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The plot is real product, not decoration. A café on its own 500+ sq. yd of open land has an experience (space, air, green) that a mall or high-street café cannot copy, so the "first five minutes" can be different by design.
- The card ties the whole experience to one system that the business owns: entry, payment, identity and follow-up. That is the raw material for owning every touchpoint end to end.
- The members-only gate lets the café control crowding and quality, which fits the promise of clean, calm and uncluttered.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No one thing.** The pitch is a list of eleven values (eco, green, sustainable, clean, pure, smiles, happiness, creativity, freedom, community, exclusive) and no product. The food concept, menu and hours are all unknown. Without a single thing it does brilliantly, the membership is a fee for a vague mood, and the fee will not survive the first visit.
2. **The card is the product's front door, and it is a wall.** Before a member tastes anything they must understand a joining fee, a load and a minimum spend, with six candidate structures still open. The first five minutes are paperwork and a payment decision, not coffee. Every extra rule (expiry, deductions, guest access, refunds) is a seam where the experience breaks and the member feels penalised.
3. **One card for a 19-year-old student and a 46-year-old professional.** These people want different hours, prices, noise levels and reasons to come. Serving both with one space risks a place that is perfect for neither. Tier designs (structure F) add complexity rather than focus.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence, what does this café do better than anywhere else in Ahmedabad?
- Walk me through a new member's first visit, from the gate to the first sip. Where is the card handed over, loaded and explained?
- If you had to cut to one age group and one occasion (work, date, family, creating), which?
- What is the signature item a member would bring a friend for?
- What does "sustainable" mean on the plate, in the build and in the bill, in ways a member can see?
- Open questions, not answered here: whether members will pay a fee to get in at all, and at what price (/founder-consumer, then /founder-pricing); what the build, land and running costs are and what minimum spend they force (/founder-cfo); which cafés near Thaltej already own the "green, calm" position (/founder-competitors).

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. A one-sentence product statement and a defined menu concept with one signature offering.
2. A written first-visit journey with the card reduced to one simple structure a new member can understand in under a minute (no monthly penalty at launch).
3. One primary segment chosen for launch, with the others deferred.
4. Evidence from /founder-consumer that the target segment will pay to join, before any build spend.
