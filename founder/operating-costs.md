# Operating costs to know before launch: Green Card Café, Thaltej

A checklist of every cost to price **before** opening. Get a written quote for
each line, enter it in `founder/operating-costs.csv`, and `/founder-cfo` turns it
into margin, break-even and the real joining fee and minimum recharge.

Percentages marked *rule of thumb* are common restaurant-industry planning
ranges, not quotes for Ahmedabad. Replace them with your own quotes.

**Two Gujarat facts that change the maths:**
- **Gujarat is a dry state:** no alcohol sales. Margin has to come from food,
  coffee and drinks, events and the card float, not from a bar.
- **Restaurant GST is generally 5% with no input tax credit,** so GST paid on
  rent, equipment and supplies is a real cost, not recoverable. Confirm with
  your CA.

## B. Monthly running costs
### 1. Food & beverage cost (variable)
- Ingredients, coffee beans, milk, bakery inputs. *Rule of thumb: 28–35% of food
  and drink sales.*
- **Eco packaging** (compostable cups, boxes, straws) costs more than plastic.
  Get quotes.
- **Wastage and staff meals.** Plan for 2–4% of sales.

### 2. People (usually the biggest fixed cost)
List each role with a headcount per shift (opening 7am–11pm needs about two shifts):
- Café manager, assistant manager / shift lead
- Head chef, line cooks, kitchen helpers, dishwashers
- Baristas, servers (members) and servers (non-member zone)
- Card desk / reception / membership host
- Housekeeping, gardener, **security + parking marshal** (25 cars)
- Event / community coordinator (the "creativity, community" promise)
- Add-ons: **PF, ESI, Gujarat professional tax**, bonus, overtime, uniforms,
  staff food, staff accommodation if any, hiring and attrition costs

*Rule of thumb: total staff cost 20–30% of sales.*

### 3. Land & premises
- Rent / lease (with yearly escalation), or loan EMI if you bought
- **AMC property tax**
- Building and garden maintenance reserve

### 4. Utilities
- **Electricity** (commercial tariff; AC and kitchen load, less solar savings)
- **Gas** (piped gas or commercial LPG cylinders)
- **Water** (municipal, borewell or tankers in summer; the garden adds a lot)
- Internet (business line plus a backup)

### 5. Repairs & maintenance (AMCs)
- AC, kitchen equipment, coffee machine, cold room, RO, DG set, solar panels
- Fire extinguisher refills, CCTV, POS/card readers
- **Pest control (monthly; needed for FSSAI hygiene)**, grease-trap cleaning,
  deep cleaning
- Furniture and swing upkeep, outdoor cushions (sun and monsoon damage)

### 6. Technology & payments
- POS and card-system subscription, member app / CRM, accounting software
- **Payment charges:** UPI is usually free; cards about 1.5–2% (check with your
  bank)
- **Physical card cost** per new member (recover it through the card fee)
- Music streaming licence for business use, plus PPL/IPRS licences

### 7. Marketing & community
- Social media management, content shoots, ads
- Member events: artists, workshop materials, movie-night licences
- Founding-member perks (free drink each month, guest passes): **cost them at
  food cost**

### 8. Admin & compliance
- CA / GST filing, payroll, legal retainer
- Licence renewals (FSSAI, trade licence, fire NOC), insurance premiums
  (fire/property, public liability, equipment, group health)
- Bank charges, loan interest

### 9. Waste & sustainability
- Wet-waste composting, dry-waste recycling vendor, used-oil collection
- Garden: plants, manure, seasonal replanting

### 10. Contingency
- 5–10% of monthly costs for things that break or get missed

## C. Money that is not a cost but must be planned
- **Card float liability:** all unspent balance on members' cards is owed in
  food. Keep enough cash to serve it, and don't spend it on the build.
- **GST on sales** collected and paid monthly. It is not your income.
- **Seasonality:** Ahmedabad summers (open-air seating less usable) and the
  monsoon. Plan lower months.

## D. The five numbers to bring to `/founder-cfo`
1. Land: monthly rent or EMI
2. Total one-time setup (A1–A14)
3. Monthly staff cost (from the role list)
4. Monthly fixed costs (B3–B10)
5. Average spend per visit and visits per member per month (from the panel or a pre-sale)
