A 4/10 FUND IF means: there is a business here, but not yet in the form it was
pitched. Do not spend on the build until the conditions below are met.

## Risks raised by more than one member
1. **No single thing it does best (all three).** Eleven values are listed, but
   there is no product, outcome or reason to choose it over the café down the road.
   The Offers lens calls it "a commodity with a gate", the Monopoly lens "nothing
   proprietary", and the Product lens "no one thing".
2. **The card asks too much before anyone has proof (Offers, Product).** A fee you
   cannot spend, a locked balance and a minimum-spend penalty, all for a café that
   does not exist yet. The first five minutes are paperwork.
3. **19–46 is two or more different customers (all three).** A student and a
   42-year-old professional want different hours, prices and noise levels.
4. **Unknown competition near Thaltej (Offers, Monopoly).** If garden or
   "aesthetic" cafés already own this buyer, members-only becomes a barrier to
   you, not an advantage.
5. **No plan for the first 100 members (Monopoly, Offers).** How they arrive,
   what each one costs, and what proof convinces them to prepay.

## Where the members disagree
- **Tiers.** The Offers lens likes the bonus ladder (C) and segment tiers (F) as a
  good/better/best stack. The Product lens says tiers add complexity and wants one
  simple structure a new member understands in under a minute. The Monopoly lens
  is neutral on tiers and cares only that the founding cohort fills.
- **Where the moat is.** The Monopoly lens sees the land and the community as the
  long-term asset. The Product lens sees the open-air plot as the product itself
  (an experience a mall café cannot copy). The Offers lens sees no moat in either
  until there is a sharp outcome.

Still unknown and needed by `/founder-cfo`: budget, and whether the land is owned
or leased.

## The strongest version the board can see
This differs from the pitch. It is not "an exclusive café with good values". It is
**a green open-air creative clubhouse for one clear crowd**, for example
Ahmedabad's young creators and professionals who want somewhere calm and outdoor
to work, meet and make things. It has one signature menu idea, and a card that is
simply prepaid credit with a small founding bonus. It opens only after a capped
founding round of members has paid in, proving demand and funding part of the
build. The open land is the product (a space no mall café can copy), and the
member community, with events, a founders' wall and members bringing members, is
what makes it hard to copy. Exclusivity comes from the cap, not from penalties.
