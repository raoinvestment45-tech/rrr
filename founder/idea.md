# Green Card Café (working name)

- What it is: An eco, green, sustainable members-only café on its own open land (minimum 500 sq. yards, about 4,500 sq. ft) built around cleanliness, purity, happiness, creativity, freedom and community.
- Who it is for: Members aged 19 to 46 in and around Thaltej, Ahmedabad.
- What it sells, at what price: Café food and drinks, paid for only through a stored-value membership card. The card works like a gaming-zone balance card: a small one-time card fee, then recharge packs with bonus credit on bigger recharges, a minimum recharge amount and a validity period on the balance. You need the card to get in, and every purchase is deducted from it. The exact figures are not decided yet; see `founder/membership-structures.md`.
- Where and how: Near Thaltej, Ahmedabad. Physical café on its own plot. Exclusive: only card members (and possibly their guests) can enter and buy.
- Budget and constraints: Budget not given yet. Whether the land is owned or leased is not stated yet. Minimum plot size 500 sq. yards.
